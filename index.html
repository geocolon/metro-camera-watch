      Before you drive

      • Set everything up while parked. Don't touch your phone while driving: alerts are spoken out loud.
      • Mount your phone and keep this page open on screen. Phones pause web pages that are locked or in the background, and then alerts stop.
      • Camera data comes from volunteers and the city. Some cameras are missing or out of date, so always follow posted speed limits.
      • The page doesn't save or upload your location. Map tiles and live camera lookups for your area come from OpenStreetMap servers, which see the area shown, like any online map.
